    Cardiovascular Disease Prediction

    Cardiovascular diseases (CVDs) remain a major global health threat, contributing significantly to mortality and disability rates. Accurate and early risk prediction is essential for preventive healthcare. This research emphasizes the application of deep learning-based stacking classifiers to improve clinical decision-making and enhance personalized treatment strategies using structured medical data.

    Learn more about us

    Problem Statement

    Traditional single-model classifiers often fail to detect complex cardiovascular disease patterns, leading to suboptimal risk prediction and delayed diagnosis. There is a need for advanced ensemble methods that can integrate diverse learning capabilities to improve the reliability and accuracy of cardiovascular risk assessment, especially with structured clinical datasets.

    Objective of the Project

    The main objective is to develop a stacking ensemble model combining multiple classifiers—including XGBoost—for effective cardiovascular disease prediction. The goal is to improve model accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score through ensemble learning, ultimately contributing to the development of clinical decision support systems and personalized patient care approaches.
